## Project Overview
Christ College of Engineering (CCE) Digital Outpass System.
A secure, multi-role outpass management and gate verification platform enabling students to request outpasses, faculty (tutors & HODs) to approve/reject them, and security guards to scan rotating cryptographic QR passes at campus gates.
